What is an Algorithm?

Objective: Understand what an algorithm is and why step-by-step instructions matter. This lesson includes an animated explanation, an interactive 3D simulation, and a short quiz to check understanding.

Animated Explanation

Watch the short animated video below. Captions are available on the video player (YouTube).

Quick explanation (short)

An algorithm is a clear set of steps or instructions you follow to solve a problem or complete a task — for example, following steps to make a sandwich or to sort a list of numbers. Algorithms can be written in words, diagrams, or code.

Why 3D demos?

3D simulations make abstract concepts tangible. For algorithms, you can visualize sorting, searching, and step-by-step transformations. Use presets and parameters to test different scenarios.
